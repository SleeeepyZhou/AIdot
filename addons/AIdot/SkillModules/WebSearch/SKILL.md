---
name: WebSearch
description: Search the web for current information
tool_names: web_search
---

Use `web_search` when you need information that may be more recent than your training data, or when the user asks about real-world events, facts, or data.

Formulate a concise, specific search query. Summarize the most relevant results in your answer rather than quoting them verbatim.

Do not call `web_search` more than 3 times per conversation turn.
